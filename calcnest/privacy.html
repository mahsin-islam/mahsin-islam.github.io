Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 July 2026 · Applies to the CalcNest Android app (“CalcNest”, “the app”)

This policy explains what information CalcNest uses, how advertising and in-app purchases work, and the choices you have. The short version: CalcNest does not require an account and keeps your calculations, history, and preferences on your device. Advertising is provided by Google AdMob and is only requested after you have made your consent choice where required.

1. Information we collect

CalcNest works offline and is designed to minimise data collection.

  • Data you create — your calculation history (the most recent entries), any favourites you star, and your settings (theme, accent colour, AMOLED mode, haptics) are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to us.
  • No account — there is no sign-in, profile, or identifier that ties your activity to you on our systems.
  • Advertising data — to show ads, Google AdMob may collect or use device and usage signals (such as an advertising identifier, coarse/non-precise location, IP address, and app activity) in line with Google’s policies and your consent choice. See Section 3.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

CalcNest is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob, which may include banner, interstitial, app-open, rewarded, and native formats. Google may use the data it collects to personalise ads. Google’s privacy practices apply to this data:

  • How Google uses data from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob & data use

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app shows a Google consent form (User Messaging Platform) on first launch and only requests ads after you have granted consent or if consent is not required for you. You can change your choice any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset your device’s advertising ID or delete it in your Android settings, which limits ad personalisation.

3. In-app purchases

CalcNest offers an optional one-time Remove ads purchase. Payments are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not see or store your payment-card details; those are handled by Google Play according to the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play’s terms.

4. Permissions the app requests

CalcNest requests only the permissions it needs:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to load advertisements. The calculator and tools themselves work offline.
  • BILLING — to support the optional in-app purchase.
  • Device vibration — used only for optional haptic feedback; no data is transmitted.

CalcNest does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, SMS, precise location, or photo/file storage.

5. How your data is stored and deleted

The data you create in CalcNest stays on your phone or tablet. It is removed when you uninstall the app. Advertising and purchase data held by Google is governed by Google’s policies; you can manage or delete it through your Google Account.

6. Children’s privacy

CalcNest is a general-purpose utility app and is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. AdMob ad serving follows Google’s families and content-rating requirements.

7. Your privacy choices

  • Limit ad personalisation — change your consent choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices, or reset/opt out of the advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Remove ads — purchase Remove ads to stop ad serving in the app.
  • Delete your data — uninstall CalcNest to erase all locally stored history, favourites, and settings.
  • Exercise your rights — where laws such as the GDPR or CCPA apply, you may request access to or erasure of data; note that the app holds no server-side account data about you.

8. Third-party links

The app may open external links (for example, this policy, the terms, or an email compose window for support). Those destinations are operated by others and have their own privacy practices.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The “effective date” above reflects the latest version. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
