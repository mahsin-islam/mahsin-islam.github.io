## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Case Studies Are Your Star**
   - Make them detailed & specific
   - Show real metrics (revenue, users, performance)
   - Explain the problem, solution, and impact
   - Add links to live demos when possible

2. **Keep Analytics Private**
   - Don't share client metrics publicly
   - Use anonymized/general data
   - Focus on your role & contributions

3. **Update Regularly**
   - Add new case studies quarterly
   - Update projects as they evolve
   - Refresh metrics monthly
   - Keep blog posts linked

4. **Promote Strategically**
   - Share on LinkedIn
   - Link from Medium articles
   - Mention in YouTube videos
   - Get backlinks from tech sites

5. **Track Everything**
   - Monitor GA4 dashboard
